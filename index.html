Air Chords

Play chords with your hands through your webcam. Hold up fingers to pick a chord, then sing over it. Works in any key, and with any song's chords.

How to play

  • Hold up N fingers (both hands add up) to play chord N. Thumbs count too.
  • Fists or hands down = silence.
  • Pick a key to get its 7 chords, or paste any song's chords (from a chord sheet, any format). Up to 10 chords, one per finger.
  • Pick a sound: 4 built-in synths, or any of the 128 standard instruments (piano, guitars, bass, strings, choir, brass, sax, flute, sitar…). Each one loads the first time you pick it.
  • Hold each shape for a moment; the chord changes once your hand is steady.
